How alerts work

  1. Stock reaches or falls below its restock threshold.
  2. The system creates a pending alert.
  3. The email service sends a notification to the saved recipients.

Editing this list does not send an email. Emails go out automatically once a sender account is set up on the server.

INSIGHTS & ACTIVITY

Reports

View inventory reports and operational insights.

Reports run against live data. The date range applies to the usage, history and giveaway reports.

Available Reports

Current Inventory Report

View merchandise items, variants, current quantities, setpoints, and available monetary values.

Low Inventory / Restock Report

Identify items at or below their setpoints and review inventory levels and alert statuses.

Inventory Usage Report

Track merchandise distributed during selected periods, including quantities and usage by category.

Inventory Value Report

Review inventory quantities and total values for items with available monetary information.

Transaction History Report

Review Stock In and Stock Out history, including dates, users, merchandise, quantities, and transaction details.

Giveaway / Relationship Gift Report

Review merchandise distributed together, recipient context, distribution category, event details, and quantities.

Demand / Forecasting Report

Analyze historical merchandise usage to identify frequently distributed items and potential replenishment needs.

Weekly Security Review

Deleted transactions, changes made outside the app, staff and alert-email changes, and data integrity checks for the last 7 days.
